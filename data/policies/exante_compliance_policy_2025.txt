Document Version: 2025.1

Last Updated: January 15, 2025

SECTION 1: CUSTOMER ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
---------------------------------------------

1.1 Age Requirements by Product Type

The following minimum age requirements apply to all customer accounts:

- Standard Stock Trading Account: Minimum age 18 years
- ETF Trading Account: Minimum age 18 years
- Mutual Funds Account: Minimum age 18 years
- Futures Trading Account: Minimum age 21 years
- Options Trading Account: Minimum age 21 years
- Margin Trading Account: Minimum age 21 years
- Forex Trading Account: Minimum age 21 years
- Cryptocurrency Trading Account: Minimum age 25 years
- High-Risk Derivatives Account: Minimum age 25 years

Note: Age is calculated from the date of birth as stated on the official identity document to the current date.

1.2 Identity Document Requirements

All customers must provide valid government-issued identification:

- Accepted documents: KTP (Kartu Tanda Penduduk), Passport, or SIM (Surat Izin Mengemudi)
- Document must be valid and not expired at the time of application
- Document that has been expired for more than 30 days will not be accepted
- All information on the document must be clearly legible
- Document must contain: Full name, identification number, date of birth, and expiration date

1.3 Document Expiration Policy

- Documents expired within 30 days: Application pending, customer must provide new document
- Documents expired more than 30 days: Application rejected, customer must renew document first
- Documents with "SEUMUR HIDUP" (lifetime validity): Always accepted if other criteria met


SECTION 2: PRODUCT-SPECIFIC RESTRICTIONS
----------------------------------------

2.1 Futures Trading Restrictions

Futures trading carries significant risk and is restricted to:
- Customers aged 21 years or older
- Customers with valid, non-expired identity documents
- Customers who have completed risk acknowledgment form

Customers under 21 years of age are NOT eligible for Futures trading under any circumstances.

2.2 Cryptocurrency Trading Restrictions

Due to the volatile nature of cryptocurrency markets:
- Minimum age requirement: 25 years
- Additional verification required for transactions over $10,000
- Customers must complete cryptocurrency risk assessment

2.3 Margin and Leverage Restrictions

Margin trading and leveraged products require:
- Minimum age: 21 years
- Minimum account balance: $5,000
- Completion of margin trading agreement


SECTION 3: COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION PROCESS
------------------------------------------

3.1 Automated Verification Steps

1. Document authenticity verification
2. Age calculation and eligibility check
3. Product restriction validation
4. Sanctions and PEP screening

3.2 Manual Review Triggers

The following conditions require manual compliance review:
- Customer age is within 1 year of minimum requirement
- Document quality is poor or partially illegible
- Mismatched information between documents
- High-risk country of residence


SECTION 4: REGIONAL RESTRICTIONS
--------------------------------

4.1 Indonesia-Specific Requirements

For Indonesian customers (identified by NIK on KTP):
- NIK must be 16 digits
- Must be Indonesian citizen or permanent resident
- Subject to OJK (Otoritas Jasa Keuangan) regulations

4.2 Cross-Border Trading

International customers may face additional restrictions based on their country of residence and applicable regulations.

SECTION 6: POLICY VIOLATIONS
----------------------------

6.1 Consequences of Non-Compliance

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Account suspension or termination
- Reporting to regulatory authorities
- Legal action if fraud is suspected

6.2 Appeals Process

Customers may appeal eligibility decisions by:
1. Submitting additional documentation
2. Requesting manual review
3. Contacting compliance department
